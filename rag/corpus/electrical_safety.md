# Electrical Safety Manual

### 1 Scope
This manual applies to all work on or near energized electrical equipment above 50 volts, including panel work, temporary power distribution, and generator tie-ins on IronBridge sites.

### 2 Lockout/Tagout
Before any electrical maintenance, the affected circuit must be de-energized and locked with a single-worker-owned lock and tag. Group lockout requires a lockout box with one lock per crew member. No circuit may be re-energized until every applicable lock has been removed by its owner.

### 3 Arc Flash PPE
Workers performing tasks within the arc flash boundary must wear PPE rated for the calculated incident energy at that panel, as listed on the equipment's arc flash label. Minimum PPE for unknown or unlabeled equipment is Category 4 until an engineering study is completed.

### 4.1 Voltage Testing
A qualified electrician must test for absence of voltage using a rated meter immediately before beginning work, testing each phase to ground and phase to phase. The meter itself must be tested on a known live source before and after use to confirm it is functioning.

### 4.2b Grounding Confirmation
After voltage testing confirms a de-energized state, temporary protective grounds must be applied at the work location in accordance with the equipment manufacturer's grounding points. Grounds must be visually verified as connected before any conductor is touched, and removed only after work is complete and the lockout is released.
