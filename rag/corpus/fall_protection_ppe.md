# Fall Protection & PPE Manual

### 1 Scope
This manual applies to any work performed at a height of 6 feet or more above a lower level, including roofing, steel erection, and elevated platform work on IronBridge sites.

### 2 Anchor Points
Fall arrest anchor points must be engineered or manufacturer-rated for a minimum of 5,000 pounds per attached worker, and must be independent of any scaffolding or guardrail system unless that system is separately rated for fall arrest loads.

### 3 Harness Inspection
Full-body harnesses and lanyards must be inspected by the wearer before every use and by a competent person monthly. Any harness that has arrested a fall must be removed from service immediately and tagged out, regardless of visible damage.

### 4 Fall Clearance Calculation
Before connecting to an anchor, the worker must calculate total fall clearance, including free fall distance, deceleration distance, harness stretch, and a safety margin, to confirm the lower level will not be struck. Undersized clearance requires a shorter lanyard or a self-retracting lifeline instead.

### 5 Rescue Plan
A site-specific rescue plan must be in place and communicated to the crew before any work begins where fall arrest equipment may be used, since a suspended worker can develop suspension trauma within minutes. Rescue must be capable of retrieving a suspended worker within 15 minutes of a fall.
