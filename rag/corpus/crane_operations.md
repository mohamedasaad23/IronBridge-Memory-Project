# Crane Operations Manual

### 1 Scope
This manual governs all mobile and tower crane operations on IronBridge job sites, including pre-operation inspection, load handling, signal communication, and emergency response. It applies to certified crane operators, riggers, signal persons, and site supervisors.

### 2 Pre-Operation Inspection
Before every shift, the operator must complete a documented inspection covering wire rope condition, hydraulic lines, outrigger pads, and boom angle indicators. Any defect classified as a "stop-work" item halts operation until a qualified inspector signs off. Inspection logs are retained for 3 years.

### 3 Load Limits
Crane load charts must be matched to the exact boom configuration and radius in use for the lift. Operators may never exceed 90% of rated capacity without a documented engineering variance. Multi-crane lifts require a written lift plan reviewed by a qualified rigger prior to any load movement.

### 4 Signal Communication
Only one designated signal person may direct a crane at a time, using standard ANSI hand signals or a dedicated radio channel. If visual contact between operator and signal person is lost for any reason, the operator must stop all crane motion immediately until contact is restored.

### 5 Emergency Procedures
In the event of power line contact, the operator must remain in the cab, warn others to stay clear, and attempt to move the crane away from the line only if it can be done safely. All crane-related incidents, including near misses, must be reported to the site safety officer within 1 hour.
