# Generator & Fuel Handling Manual

### 1 Scope
This manual covers portable and towable generators used for temporary power on IronBridge sites, including fuel storage, refueling, and spill response.

### 2 Fuel Storage
Diesel and gasoline must be stored in approved safety containers, at least 25 feet from any ignition source, occupied structure, or excavation. No more than 60 gallons of flammable fuel may be stored above ground per site without a secondary containment berm.

### 3 Refueling Procedure
Generators must be shut down and allowed to cool for at least 5 minutes before refueling. Refueling must never occur while the generator is running or near an active electrical tie-in, and a fire extinguisher rated for Class B fires must be within reach during the operation.

### 4 Spill Response
Any fuel spill larger than 1 gallon must be contained immediately with absorbent material, reported to the site safety officer, and documented regardless of whether it reaches soil or a storm drain. Spills reaching a storm drain require immediate notification of the environmental compliance lead.
