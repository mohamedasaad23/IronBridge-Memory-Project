# Excavation Safety Manual

### 1 Scope
This manual covers all trenching and excavation work deeper than 4 feet on IronBridge sites, including soil classification, protective systems, utility locates, and atmospheric monitoring.

### 2 Soil Classification
A competent person must classify soil as Type A, B, or C before any protective system is selected, using visual and manual tests. Soil that has been previously disturbed, is layered, or shows signs of seeping water is automatically downgraded to Type C regardless of other test results.

### 3 Protective Systems
Excavations 5 feet or deeper require a protective system: sloping, benching, shoring, or a trench box, sized for the classified soil type. No worker may enter an excavation without a documented protective system in place, except excavations entirely in stable rock.

### 4 Utility Locates
A utility locate request must be submitted at least 2 full business days before excavation begins, and all marked utilities must be confirmed by potholing before mechanical digging crosses within 2 feet of a marked line.

### 5 Atmospheric Testing
Excavations deeper than 4 feet where hazardous atmosphere is possible must be tested for oxygen level, flammability, and toxic gases before entry and continuously if workers remain inside for extended periods. Entry is prohibited if oxygen falls below 19.5% until forced ventilation restores safe levels.
